# Rubric: Video Evaluation (Spotify-mix TikTok)

The multidimensional replacement for a fake universal virality score. Used by evaluation skills
against fixtures now and against real edit plans in phase 3+. Each dimension is rated
weak / adequate / strong with evidence from the video/edit-spec itself.

## A. First impression (target behaviours: reduce early skip)

1. First-frame content promise: is it clear within ~1 second what this video offers?
2. Hook clarity: is the hook comprehensible on first read at mobile size, and honest about the
   content?
3. Immediate audio interest: does the opening audio moment earn the next three seconds?

## B. Retention and payoff (targets: average watch time, completion, rewatch)

4. Anticipation structure: is a payoff (the transition) promised and locatable?
5. Payoff delivery: does the transition land where anticipation peaks, without dead time?
6. Pacing: no stretch a viewer could skip without losing the promise.
7. Loop/rewatch potential: does the ending connect to the beginning or invite a second listen?

## C. The music and the niche (constraint dimensions — failure here vetoes)

8. Audio fidelity: mix quality preserved; no processing that damages the music.
9. Music centrality: overlays/motion serve the listening experience, never bury it.
10. Spotify legibility: interface readable at mobile size; "made in Spotify" is communicable.
11. Anonymous format respected: no identity elements beyond craft signals.

## D. Conversion (targets: shares, comments, follows)

12. Reason to share: who would send this to whom, and why?
13. Comment prompt: is there a genuine, on-taste conversation starter (not bait)?
14. Reason to follow: does the video signal a series/skill worth subscribing to?

## E. Integrity

15. Honesty: nothing misrepresents the content to win the first second.
16. Technical eligibility: duration, format (9:16), export quality within platform norms.

Output: per-dimension rating + note, list of concrete edit-spec changes, and explicit
"cannot assess without real data" items. Never an overall score.
