# Hooks

Revision date: 2026-08-03

The first-seconds device (text, audio moment, visual cue) that earns continued watching.

## Current conclusion (seed — thin evidence, heuristic tier)

A hook's job in this niche is to make an **honest, specific content promise** — "a transition
worth waiting four seconds for" — comprehensible at mobile size within ~1 second. The
justification chain: promise clarity → fewer early skips → higher average watch time and
completion (`claim-20260803-hyp-early-skip-promise`, hypothesis) → completion is officially a
strong ranking signal (`claim-20200618-completion-strong-signal`, fact).

Constraints that outrank any hook tactic:

- Honesty: a hook that misrepresents content (manufactured controversy, fake claims) is
  prohibited regardless of predicted attention effects (creator preference + integrity
  anti-goal; see fixture `fix-06-misleading-hook`).
- Music centrality: hook elements must clear the transition window and never obscure the
  Spotify UI (`claim-20260803-pref-music-central`, `claim-20260803-pref-spotify-legibility`).
