# Value Proposition

Revision date: 2026-08-03

## Current conclusion

The content's novelty is that **polished, DJ-like listening experiences can be created and
shared inside Spotify itself**. Videos succeed when they make three things felt within seconds:
(1) this transition is genuinely good; (2) it was made in Spotify, so (3) you can have it — add
the mix, or follow for more. The creator stays anonymous; craft is the identity
(`claim-20260803-pref-anonymous-format`).

Everything else is subordinate to the music: overlays, hooks and CTAs may never obscure the
listening experience or the Spotify interface's legibility
(`claim-20260803-pref-music-central`, `claim-20260803-pref-spotify-legibility`).

Conversion ladder implied by the brief (in priority order of the project): view → follow →
share → comment → like, with library-adds of the mixes as the niche-specific bonus outcome.
