# platform-constraints/

TikTok technical, policy and eligibility constraints affecting production — `claim_type:
constraint` territory. Constraints are preconditions, not optimisations: a video failing them may
not be recommended at all.

Currently seeded: `eligibility.md` (vertical slice). Planned (phase 2): technical export
requirements, music/rights constraints, text/safe-zone requirements.

Document contract and editing rules: `../README.md`. Who writes: agents via reviewed PRs only.
