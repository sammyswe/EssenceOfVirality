# Channel fit — Spotify house-mix hooks

Constraints that every scroll-stop brief must pass. These are creator rulings,
not TikTok folklore.

## Always true

- The mix audio is carefully made by the creator; copy never claims the
  playlist, queue, or Spotify "mixed itself".
- Music is the product; the hook is a muted overlay on already-playing mix
  audio.
- AI/Higgsfield footage is fiction: use a visible fiction signal (stylised,
  impossible, parody). No fabricated reality.
- On-screen overlay text is drawtext-safe: no colour emoji.
- Overlay text is a subjective claim or anticipation the video can keep within
  seconds — not an invented third-party reaction ("DJs hate…").

## Growth-phase asks

- Prefer comment/save CTAs; follow asks are rarer (~1 in 4).
- Milestone promises only when the creator has accepted a concrete number.

## Niche anchors

- House / speaker / pre-drinks / aux fit the account; gym stays experimental.
- The Spotify capture (labels, BPM, waveform) stays legible after the hook
  hands over — the scroll-stop must not train the viewer to expect a different
  product.

## When a technique fails channel fit

Mark `channel_fit: reject` in techniques.md (or `adapt` with the adaptation
written). Never apply a rejected technique to a brief.
